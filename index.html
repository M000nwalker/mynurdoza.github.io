01 — About

Who Am I?

Dhaka, Bangladesh

I'm a Mechanical Engineering undergraduate at BUET with a passion for pushing the boundaries of physical systems through digital modeling, simulation, and intelligent control.

From designing motor control circuits with ESP32 microcontrollers to integrating them with ROS 2 in Linux environments — I bridge hardware and software seamlessly. My work in multiphysics simulation (FEA, thermal & fluid coupling) lets me analyze and validate mechanical behaviors before a single bolt is turned.

Outside the lab, I'm an avid chess player and a curious mind always seeking new challenges — whether it's tracking celestial objects with my self-built telescope or competing on the chessboard.
